# GREEN-EYE: Arctic Climate & Maritime Intelligence Core

**Open-Source Sovereign & Environmental Intelligence Initiative**  
*Framework: Multimodal Satellite & Sensor Data Ingestion powered by Advanced Gemini AI*

---

## 🌍 Vision & Mission
The **GREEN-EYE** project establishes an autonomous, real-time ecological and geopolitical monitoring framework for Greenland and its surrounding territorial waters. By fusing high-resolution synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite data, maritime telemetry, seismic sensors, and long-endurance drone telemetry, GREEN-EYE leverages multimodal artificial intelligence to detect environmental anomalies, prevent illegal industrial or fishing activities, and predict climate-driven glacier dynamics.

---

## 🛠️ Core Technical Architecture
1. **Multimodal Ingestion Engine:** Connects live data streams from European space initiatives (Sentinel/Copernicus) and localized polar sensors.
2. **AI Processing Core (Gemini):** Cross-references visual imagery, thermal telemetry, and acoustic signatures to detect unlisted ("dark") vessels and ice-sheet fracture risks.
3. **Automated Alert Matrix:** Generates high-priority situational awareness reports for research institutions, environmental agencies, and maritime authorities.
